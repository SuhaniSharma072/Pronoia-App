Pronoia is an iOS care companion app built for older adults and their caretakers. Older adults can track medications, log their mood, manage appointments, and stay connected with 
their caretaker through real-time alerts and a native messaging integration. Caretakers get a live dashboard showing whether medications have been taken, upcoming appointments, 
mood journal entries, and instant notifications when their loved one needs attention. A gamified wellness plant grows as the elder maintains their daily medication streak, turning 
routine care into something meaningful. Built with SwiftUI and Firebase, Pronoia keeps both sides of a care relationship informed, connected, and supported.
